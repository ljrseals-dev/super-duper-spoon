# 9. What Not To Do

- Do not claim BS.1770-4 or EBU R128 measurement steps as the invention.
- Do not name the practitioner as an inventor unless she contributed to conception.
- Do not file as representative unless the preparer is a registered USPTO practitioner.
- Do not claim the 8× output as an independent claim without a search.
- Do not describe the report as “novel” without the prior-art search.
- Do not copy repository code verbatim into the specification.
- Do not let the public repo date go unrecorded.
